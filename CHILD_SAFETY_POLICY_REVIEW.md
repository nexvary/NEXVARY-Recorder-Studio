# Child/family policy review

The product is positioned for guardian-managed family safety. Adding Child Mode does **not** automatically make a declared adult-only audience correct. Google assesses actual imagery, language, functionality and intended users. Determine the real age groups and applicable markets before submitting Target audience and Content declarations.

If children are included, review Google Play Families requirements and applicable consent/data laws. Camera, microphone, authentication and location are sensitive. Google states that apps solely targeting children may not request location permission or collect/use/transmit precise location; this product's proposed child safety positioning therefore needs careful audience/product review before release. No claim of Families compliance is made.

Controls implemented: PIN protection for parent settings, contacts, billing and cloud configuration; 4-action child surface; local SOS remains outside PIN; explicit opt-in for automatic sharing; private evidence, temporary links, verified cloud recipients, limited cloud quota, manual SMS/dialer, account/evidence deletion API. No ads SDK or advertising identifier is introduced.

Privacy notice must explain: guardian email/account, child/household profile where supplied, optional medical data, optional foreground location, media evidence and timing/usage metadata; purpose; AWS region and optional Wasabi deployment; guardian recipients; transfer/access; Free/Premium retention; deletion; how consent is changed; emergency limitations; contact/support. The operator must provide legal entity/contact information and a hosted privacy policy. These cannot be invented by implementation.

Do not market this as covert monitoring. The visible app, guardian consent and Android privacy indicators remain. PIN does not guarantee custody/authority and cannot replace lawful consent or policy review.

Official source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335
